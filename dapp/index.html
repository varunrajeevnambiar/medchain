Pillars of the Protocol

Designed for clinical compliance and uncompromised privacy.

01

Client-Side Encryption

Records are symmetrically encrypted using AES-256 directly within the user's browser before transmission. Server nodes and database administrators possess zero visibility into plaintext clinical narratives or diagnostic observations.

02

Granular Disclosure

Patients configure specific clinical scopes for each visiting specialist. Orthopedic physicians receive access to imaging and surgical records while sensitive psychiatric or reproductive notes remain strictly sealed under separate key envelopes.

03

Instant Revocation

Consent authorizations contain deterministic expiration limits and can be terminated on demand. Revoking access permanently closes the decryption gateway, instantly blocking any subsequent practitioner inquiry.

Zero-Trust Clinical Security

Cryptographic guarantees for hospital-grade data protection.

01

Zero-Knowledge Storage

Health payloads are symmetrically encrypted using AES-256 before leaving your device. Even database administrators and hosting infrastructure possess zero ability to read or tamper with medical data.

02

Crypto-Shredding (GDPR)

Fulfills GDPR Article 17 "Right to Erasure" on permanent ledgers. Deleting a record irreversibly purges its unique AES decryption key, rendering the historical ciphertext into permanent mathematical noise.

03

Audited Emergency Override

Accredited trauma physicians can initiate a Break-Glass override during life-or-death emergencies. All overrides require recorded clinical justifications and emit unforgeable alerts on the audit trail.
